How it works

  1. Connect your own accounts. Sign in to each platform on that platform's own page. Blastpack never sees your passwords; each platform gives it permission you can take back at any time.
  2. Pick the video and write the caption. Choose a video from your phone or computer, write one caption, and change it for any platform that needs something different.
  3. Preview and choose where it goes. See how it will look on each platform and switch any of them off.
  4. Confirm, then post. A confirm sheet names the profile you are posting as and shows every platform's settings. Nothing goes out until you press "Yes — blast it."
  5. Watch it land. Blastpack checks each platform until it confirms the post, and tells you plainly if one fails so you can try again — it never reports a post that did not happen.

Posting to TikTok

When TikTok is one of the destinations, the confirm sheet shows the TikTok account you are posting to, and you choose:

Who can see it

Everyone, friends, followers or only you — nothing is picked for you, and only the options TikTok allows on your account are shown.

Comments, Duet and Stitch

Allow or block each one. Anything you have turned off in TikTok stays off.

Promotional content

Disclose when a video promotes your own brand or someone else's, and TikTok labels it accordingly.

By posting to TikTok you agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation. The video is uploaded with TikTok's Content Posting API and Blastpack checks TikTok's status until the post is confirmed.

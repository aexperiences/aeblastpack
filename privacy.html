Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 August 2026.

AE Blastpack lets you publish one video to several social platforms at once. This policy explains exactly what we hold, why, and what we never do. It is written plainly on purpose.

What we store

  • Your profiles — the name, colour and optional logo you give each brand or business.
  • Platform connections — the access tokens the platforms issue when you connect an account, so we can publish on your behalf. We never see or store your platform passwords.
  • Post records — what you published, where it went, and whether each destination succeeded or failed. This is what powers the failure alerts.
  • Contact details for alerts — your email address, and a mobile number only if you choose to turn on text alerts.
Your video never sits on our servers longer than the post takes. The file you select is passed to the platforms you chose and then discarded. We do not keep a media library, we do not archive your videos, and we do not use your content to train anything.

Phone numbers

A phone number is never required to use AE Blastpack. Push and email alerts are the default and cost you nothing. If you do add a number, it is used for exactly one purpose: telling you that a post failed or that a connection is about to expire. It is never sold, never used for marketing, and never shared with the social platforms or any third party. Turning text alerts off deletes it.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data. There is no advertising business here.
  • We do not read your private messages, followers, or direct messages.
  • We do not post anything you did not explicitly confirm on the confirmation screen.
  • We do not share data between your profiles, or with other AE Blastpack customers.

Platform data

When you connect TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube or X, we request only the permissions needed to publish a post and to report whether it worked. Our use of information received from those APIs follows each platform's developer terms and limited-use requirements. You can disconnect any account at any time from the Accounts screen, which revokes our access and deletes the stored token.

Where it lives, and for how long

Profile settings are stored on your own device. Connection tokens and post records are held only as long as the connected account remains connected. Delete a profile and its connections, tokens and post history go with it.

Children

AE Blastpack is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, we will say so in the app rather than quietly editing this page.

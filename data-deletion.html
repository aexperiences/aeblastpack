Data Deletion Instructions

AE Blastpack · Accelerated Experiences LLC · Last updated 17 August 2026

AE Blastpack runs no user database. Your access tokens live in an encrypted, HttpOnly cookie in your own browser — so in most cases deleting your data takes one click and happens instantly.

Option 1 — Disconnect inside Blastpack (instant)

Open AE Blastpack, find the connected account, and choose Disconnect. The stored token is removed from your browser immediately and all access ends at that moment.

Option 2 — Revoke from Facebook or Instagram (instant)

  1. Go to Facebook → Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Find AE Blastpack in the list.
  3. Choose Remove.

Meta revokes access at that moment and any token we hold stops working instantly. For TikTok, the equivalent is Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions.

Option 3 — Email us (within 30 days)

To have a temporarily staged video file deleted, email anthonye@aexperiences.studio with the subject line Data Deletion Request. Include the Page name or Instagram handle you connected so we can locate the file.

We delete everything associated with the request within 30 days and reply in writing to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • The access token held in your browser cookie.
  • The Facebook Page and Instagram account identifiers stored alongside it.
  • Any video file staged for delivery to Meta.
  • Your local post history for that profile.

What does not get deleted

Posts already published to your own accounts. Those belong to you, they live on your account, and only you can remove them from the platform itself.
